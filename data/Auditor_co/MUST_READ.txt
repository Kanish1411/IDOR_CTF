ABOUT AUDITOR CO

Founded in 2011 by Richard Voss, a former financial regulator turned
independent auditor, Auditor Co began as a two-person practice out of a
small office focused on mid-market compliance reviews. Voss partnered
early with Elena Marsh, a forensic accountant whose work on cross-border
reporting standards helped the firm land its first major contracts in
2014.

By 2019, Auditor Co had expanded into full-scope financial and
operational audits, adding a dedicated technology-audit division led by
Priya Nandakumar, who joined from a regional risk-consulting firm and
built out the team now responsible for our systems and access-control
reviews.

As of 2026, Auditor Co is recognized as the second-largest independent
auditing firm in our operating region, trailing only one of the
long-established "big name" firms. Our growth has been driven by a
reputation for thorough, no-shortcuts reviews and a willingness to take
on clients that larger firms consider too complex or too sensitive.

Leadership today:

CLOSING NOTE

Auditor Co's growth over the past fifteen years reflects a simple
philosophy: audits should be rigorous enough that clients trust the
result, whether the news is good or bad. As we continue to expand our
technology and systems audit practice, we remain committed to the same
standard that built this firm - independence, thoroughness, and
discretion.
